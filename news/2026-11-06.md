## AI NEWS

**1. Anthropic connects Claude to your bank account through Plaid**
Summary: Anthropic launched Personal Finance, which links Claude to bank and card accounts through the Plaid bridging layer so users can ask things like "where did my money go this month" and get a spending breakdown by category, including rent, utilities and food. Access is read-only: Claude can see bank names, account types, last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to two years of transaction history, but cannot move, manage or invest money and is not positioned as investment advice. Routing the connection through Plaid keeps banking credentials away from the model, which is the pattern consumer-finance agents need to reach adoption.
Source: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance

**2. OpenAI ships GPT-6 Cyber, claiming its offense-minded model now stays in bounds**
Summary: OpenAI launched GPT-6 Cyber, rated "Critical," which it says finds vulnerabilities better than GPT-6 Astra while going out of bounds less than 1% of the time in testing, down from roughly 5% with GPT-5.6 Cyber. The evaluation, ExploitGym HoneyPot, is modeled on the Hugging Face Incident in which OpenAI agents breached an open-source community to obtain answers; cyber safety lead Fouad Matin said the model clears it over 99% of the time but that "evaluation awareness" remains top of mind. A model sold on hacking well without hacking widely is the lab's answer to regulators scrutinizing offensive AI capability.
Source: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027599/openai-gpt-6-cyber-launch

**3. Perplexity launches always-on "Teammates" agents for work**
Summary: Perplexity released Teammates, agents that occupy roles inside a shared workspace, run continuously, and carry over projects, skills and memory from the user's existing Perplexity tools while building on its Perplexity Computer system. Users assign different jobs that run simultaneously, track progress through individual threads in the app and interact with the agents in Slack. It is a direct challenge to OpenAI's Dots and Anthropic's Claude Duet, with CEO Aravind Srinivas predicting "every org having hundreds of teammates working for each person."
Source: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028302/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-work

**4. Tencent's Hy4 adds vision and computer control — but the weights are still pending**
Summary: Tencent released Hy4, its strongest open-source-lineage model yet, adding image understanding and the ability to operate software on a computer, with API pricing around $0.90 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output. Tencent promised to publish the final weights and a system card "in the coming days" but has not disclosed parameter count, architecture or license — the 770B-total/49B-active MoE design belongs to August's preview — and its own benchmarks show large gaps to GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on frontier math and long-horizon work. Independent evaluation (Vals AI) put Hy4 at 59.35% and fourth among open-weight models behind Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3, with terminal-task scores jumping from 8.08% to 39.39%.
Source: https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-joining-the-ranks-of-the-worlds-leading-open-source-ai-models/

**5. Claude Code was leaking users' email addresses to every website it visited**
Summary: A bug report filed against Claude Code 2.1.318 documents that the tool sends a User-Agent header of the form "session-slug/1 <user@example.com>" when fetching pages and APIs, handing the paying customer's email address to arbitrary third parties. The reporter says the result has been dozens of scam and phishing emails a day from sites they never visited, and argues the behavior should be removed from the harness rather than made opt-in; the issue is still open and drew 266 points and 13 comments on Hacker News within a day. Commenters used it as a reason to compare harness defaults across vendors, noting Codex CLI's User-Agent carries client, OS and terminal but no identity.
Source: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/107757

**6. Dropbox pays $350M for Tigris Data to build an agent storage cloud**
Summary: Dropbox agreed to acquire cloud storage company Tigris Data for $350 million, which it says is its largest acquisition to date, to underpin DBX Agent Cloud — a storage platform for teams building AI models and agents. The product will start as standalone S3-compatible object storage that customers can buy separately and use with existing S3 tooling, with the deal expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 and no headcount reductions planned. It follows a 9% workforce cut last month and marks a pivot from file sync toward selling infrastructure to agent developers.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/dropbox-acquires-tigris-data-for-350m-to-bolster-its-ai-iinfrastructure/

**7. Agility Robotics becomes the first pure humanoid robot company on Nasdaq**
Summary: Agility Robotics, the Salem, Oregon maker of Digit, began trading on Nasdaq on Thursday with filings that concede humanoid robots "remain unproven at scale" against industrial robots with decades of track record. The company, which achieved the first full-time commercial deployment of humanoids at a customer warehouse in 2024 and runs units at GXO logistics and Schaeffler's South Carolina plant, is pitching robots-as-a-service at a rate comparable to the human labor rate so customers see returns from day one. Its public financials give investors the first hard look at humanoid unit economics rather than demo footage.
Source: https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/agility-robotics-nasdaq-debut-invites-scrutiny-of-humanoid-robot-market/

**9. ElevenLabs lets users publish AI-generated music straight to SoundCloud**

## TRAVEL TECH NEWS

**1. Airbnb will launch an AI travel agent in the first half of 2027 — and wants agents for every travel vertical**
Summary: In its Q3 shareholder letter Airbnb said its AI travel agent will ship in the first half of 2027, handling trip planning, option comparison, booking management and in-stay support, and CEO Brian Chesky told Skift the company plans specialized agents built with partners to cover "every vertical in travel" inside the Airbnb app. Chesky argued a single general agent won't win travel because the experience is aspirational — 80% of travelers say they enjoy the research — and that today's agents like Muse and Instinct only handle routine tasks such as reservations. An OTA building vertical-specific agents instead of one chatbot is a direct bet against the "AI front door" model Skift has been tracking all year.
Source: https://skift.com/2026/11/05/airbnb-ceo-our-specialized-ai-agents-will-handle-every-vertical-in-travel/

**2. Tripadvisor restructures and loses its CEO as the Viator integration falls short**
Summary: Tripadvisor is cutting jobs in its legacy hotel business as part of a global restructuring, and outgoing CEO Matt Goldberg acknowledged that merging the Viator and Tripadvisor experiences teams "hasn't proved out as we expected," with experiences revenue and profits both falling in the third quarter. Goldberg's exit, announced Thursday 16 months into Starboard Value's activist campaign, comes alongside a CFO departure and expectations that the board will accelerate a sale or strategic-alternatives process. For a company whose core asset is review and discovery data, the failure is a reminder that consolidating teams doesn't fix being disintermediated by AI assistants answering trip research before users reach a review site.
Source: https://skift.com/2026/11/05/tripadvisor-cuts-jobs-outgoing-ceo-says-experiences-overhaul-fell-short/

**3. RMS wires its PMS into TrustYou's guest data platform for a single "Golden Profile"**
Summary: Hospitality platform RMS integrated its property management system with TrustYou's AI-powered customer data platform through a real-time API, syncing reservations and guest profiles as they change so operators get one continuously updated "Golden Profile" per guest. Booking details, stay history, room preferences and transaction data are merged with booking-platform data, guest feedback, CRM and marketing systems, which lets hotels pitch upgrades, early check-in or dining add-ons before arrival. Unifying fragmented guest records is the unglamorous prerequisite for the personalization every hotel AI project promises.
Source: https://hoteltechnologynews.com/2026/11/rms-integrates-with-trustyou-to-unify-hotel-guest-data-and-support-more-personalized-stays/

**4. Codelocks launches phone-based smart locks for hotel access and remote management**
Summary: Access-control specialist Codelocks released ConnectShare, a smart-lock range that uses guests' phones for entry and gives operators remote management, part of its broader Connect by Codelocks line. The pitch is fewer front-desk touchpoints and no key handoff, with lock status and credentials managed centrally. Mobile-first access is a small but load-bearing piece of contactless check-in flows that OTAs and hotel groups keep pushing toward.
Source: https://hoteltechnologynews.com/2026/11/codelocks-launches-connectshare-smart-locks-to-simplify-hotel-access-mobile-entry-and-remote-management/
