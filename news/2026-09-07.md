## AI NEWS

**1. OpenAI's chief scientist says progress could 'sustain into recursive self-improvement' — and warns no one is prepared**
Summary: In a personal essay published Sunday, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki wrote that internal results give him a "strong expectation" that rapid progress could sustain into recursive self-improvement, with systems in the next few years likely to represent capability jumps of equal or larger magnitude that increasingly drive their own development. He called this "a time that calls for extreme caution," said he is concerned no one is prepared for the consequences of rapidly rising machine intelligence, and argued that OpenAI will pursue technical alignment solutions and "unilaterally withhold further scaling as needed" — but that broader interventions are required. The essay, which also defends the importance of monitoring models' chain-of-thought reasoning, drew roughly 295 points on Hacker News.
Source: https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/

**2. OpenAI says it has reached its 'automated research intern' goal and is on track for an AI researcher by 2028**
Summary: OpenAI published measurements showing it has hit the goal, announced last fall, of an automated research intern by September 2026 — a system that completes well-defined research tasks under human direction, including work that would take a skilled researcher days — and says it is making strong progress toward an automated AI researcher by March 2028. The post reports that its researchers now use coding agents throughout the day in concurrent sessions, with usage outpacing other teams and agents handling increasingly complex tasks, which OpenAI says is "meaningfully accelerating research progress" while people still set priorities and decide whether to scale. OpenAI frames automated research as a way to cut the cost of advanced intelligence, while insisting alignment and safety measures must scale alongside capabilities.
Source: https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-view-inside-openai/

**3. Authors push back as publishers and agents claim shares of Anthropic's $1.5B copyright settlement**
Summary: Some authors expecting payouts from Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright class-action settlement say they received emails this week informing them that someone else was claiming their payments. Under the deal — which covers authors of nearly 500,000 titles at $3,000 per pirated work, split 50-50 with publishers when a book is still in print — writers are objecting on social media as publishers and literary agents stake claims they argue exceed their due. The dispute adds a distribution fight to one of the largest AI-training copyright settlements, which received final court approval in July.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/authors-push-back-as-publishers-and-agents-seek-share-of-anthropic-settlement/

**4. Travis Kalanick's Atoms is quietly steering toward robotaxis, the FT reports**
Summary: The Financial Times reports that Atoms — the Travis Kalanick startup that raised a $1.7 billion round led by Andreessen Horowitz this summer — is preparing a hiring spree and acquisitions that could make it a major autonomous-vehicle player, and has talked to Uber about how the ride-hailing company could use its robotaxi technology. Uber has already invested $100 million in Atoms and works with a long list of AV companies. Kalanick has described the venture as completing his "unfinished business," and the direction follows Atoms' earlier acquisition of autonomous-mining startup Pronto.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/06/travis-kalanicks-atoms-might-be-getting-into-the-robotaxi-business/

**1. Quest Apartment Hotels rebuilds its Quest Advantage loyalty program on Antavo's platform**
Summary: Quest Apartment Hotels — Australasia's largest apartment-hotel brand and part of The Ascott Limited — has partnered with Antavo to power the next evolution of its Quest Advantage loyalty program, integrating the platform with Quest's Snowflake data environment via APIs and webhooks so loyalty and guest data flow across the business. The setup is designed to let the brand continuously roll out new rewards, partnerships, and personalized offers, and to give on-property teams better visibility into guest preferences. It is the hotel group's latest step in a digital-transformation push aimed at strengthening direct guest relationships and franchisee value.
