**1. Anthropic's Opus 4.6 Is a Smut-Machine**

**2. Slack Code Drags AI Coding Out of the Terminal and Into the Group Chat**
Summary: Salesforce-owned Slack announced Slack Code, a new product that embeds AI coding agents — Anthropic's Claude Code, Cognition's Devin, GitHub Copilot, and Vercel's agent — directly into dedicated Slack channels where entire teams can watch, steer, review, and ship software together. It's available on any Slack plan at launch, though customers bring their own subscriptions to the partner agents. **3. Nvidia Research: The Harness, Not the Model, Is What Makes Agents Work**
Summary: Two Nvidia research pieces published Friday argue agent infrastructure now matters more than the underlying model. In one study, a custom harness with memory management and a "supervisor" component lifted Claude Opus 5 from 30% to a 100% score on the ARC-AGI-3 interactive reasoning benchmark — a result no frontier model achieved on its own. A companion paper introduces a cross-model KV cache transfer technique that maps a source model's prefilled cache into a target model, eliminating the expensive recompute tax when agentic systems hand tasks between small and large models. Together they point to harnesses, routing, and memory as the new battlegrounds of agentic AI.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/nvidia-just-showed-that-the-harness-not-the-ai-model-is-now-the-real-hero/

**10. Open-Source TTS Serving Achieves Sub-50ms Time-to-First-Audio**
Summary: Nari Labs published benchmarks showing its Qwen3-TTS 1.7B serving implementation hits sub-50ms p95 time-to-first-audio at up to 10 requests per second on a single H100 — the only one of five implementations tested (including vLLM-Omni and SGLang-Omni) to clear that bar. At roughly $2 per 1M characters, it undercuts commercial APIs like ElevenLabs ($100/1M) and Cartesia Sonic ($49/1M) by orders of magnitude. The implementation and benchmark are open-sourced, pushing frontier TTS latency into commodity territory.
Source: https://nari-labs.com/blog/qwen3-tts-speed-cost-frontier/
