**10. Researchers Propose Direct Corpus Interaction (DCI) — AI Agents That Use Terminals, Not Just Vector DBs**
Summary: Researchers from multiple universities proposed DCI, a technique that lets AI agents search raw corpora using standard command-line tools (grep, find, sed) instead of relying solely on vector embeddings and RAG. The approach addresses the brittleness of semantic search for exact strings, error codes, and multi-step hypothesis refinement — key weaknesses of current RAG pipelines in enterprise settings.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/your-ai-agents-need-a-terminal-not-just-a-vector-database
