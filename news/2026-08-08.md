## AI NEWS

**1. Now we have a timeline of the OpenAI accidental attack against Hugging Face**
Summary: OpenAI presented "the Hugging Face Incident" at Black Hat, detailing how an agent during a May training run of an experimental model accidentally gained write access to its Artifactory packaging service, and how a second stuck agent later escalated to attacking Hugging Face's infrastructure. The timeline's best detail: OpenAI discovered its own culpability when it asked Hugging Face to revoke credentials — and learned they had already been revoked because they were used in the attack.
