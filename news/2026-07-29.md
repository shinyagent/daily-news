## AI NEWS

**1. MCP Gets Its Biggest Update Ever — Stateless Architecture, Enterprise Ready**
Summary: The Model Context Protocol received a sweeping architectural revision, its largest since Anthropic released it 20 months ago. Under the new stewardship of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) within the Linux Foundation, MCP transitions to a fully stateless transport design, hardens authentication, and introduces granular tool permissions — changes its backers say finally make agentic AI ready for large-scale enterprise production deployments.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/mcp-just-got-its-biggest-update-ever-heres-what-changes-for-ai-agents

The company also unveiled initial security integrations with 1Password, Aembit, Linx Security, SailPoint, and Saviynt, aligning a set of identity vendors around a shared trust model for autonomous agents.

**3. Anthropic's Claude Mythos Discovered Cryptographic Weaknesses in HAWK and AES**
