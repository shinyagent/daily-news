## AI NEWS

**1. Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3 — Largest Open-Source Model Ever**
China's Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter model that benchmarks show performs neck-and-neck with top proprietary U.S. systems. The company promises open-weight availability by July 27, making it the largest open-source AI model released to date. Simon Willison noted that Moonshot is calling this the first "open science" frontier-scale model.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems

**2. Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati) Releases Inkling — Open-Weights Model**
Mira Murati's new venture, Thinking Machines Lab, released its first open-weights model: Inkling, a Mixture-of-Experts transformer with 975B total parameters (41B active). It's Apache-2.0 licensed and multimodal (text, images, audio), trained on 45 trillion tokens — a significant open-model release from a team with deep OpenAI heritage.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/inkling/#atom-everything

**3. AI Agent Security: 54% of Enterprises Have Had Incidents**
A VentureBeat survey of 107 enterprises found that more than half have already experienced a confirmed AI agent security incident or near-miss. Most organizations still let agents share credentials rather than giving each agent its own scoped identity, highlighting a major gap between agent deployment and security controls.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/ai/the-agent-security-gap-54-of-enterprises-have-already-had-an-ai-agent-incident-and-most-still-let-agents-share-credentials

**4. Google Vids Now Lets You Star in Your Own AI Videos**
Google added personalized AI avatars to Google Vids, allowing users to create videos featuring a digital version of themselves. The update also includes Gemini Omni-powered tools for generating and editing videos from prompts and reference images, pushing generative video creation further into the mainstream.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-vids-now-lets-you-star-in-your-own-ai-videos/

**5. Google's AI Mode Expands to Link and Interact with Apps**
Google is expanding its AI Mode in Search beyond answering questions into completing tasks across commonly used apps. **6. DoorDash Launches CLI for AI Agents**
DoorDash opened a limited beta of dd-cli, a command-line tool that lets developers and AI agents search stores, build carts, and place orders from the terminal. It's a notable example of software platforms building interfaces designed specifically for AI agents rather than human users.

**7. LM Studio Bionic — AI Agent for Open Models**
LM Studio launched "Bionic," an AI agent designed specifically for running open-weight models locally. It reached #13 on Hacker News with 125 points, reflecting strong community interest in local, open-model agent capabilities as an alternative to cloud-based AI agents.
Source: https://lmstudio.ai/blog/introducing-lm-studio-bionic

**8. Ring-Zero: Scaling Zero RL to a Trillion Parameters for Emergent Reasoning**
A new paper on arXiv (2607.12395) presents Ring-Zero, a reinforcement learning approach that scales to a trillion parameters for emergent reasoning capabilities. It was among the most interesting AI research hits on Hacker News today, signaling continued progress in RL-based reasoning at scale.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12395

**9. Detecting LLM-Generated Text with Classical Machine Learning**

**10. The Agent Evaluation Gap: Most Ship to Production Despite Failures**
VentureBeat's survey of 157 enterprises found that half have shipped an AI agent that passed internal evaluations but then failed in production. Only 5% fully trust their automated evaluations, revealing a significant reality-alignment problem in enterprise AI deployment.
