**1. Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a "public benefit fund"**
Summary: Anthropic said it is creating a public benefit fund and committing 5% of its shares to it, structured as a wholly-owned entity inside the company, with an option to add another 2% for every 50% increase in Anthropic's value. It floated "large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations" as possible uses, on top of the $200 million it has already committed to studying AI's effect on jobs, and said structure and governance details will come "in the coming months." The announcement lands ahead of a planned IPO and amid public sentiment that CEO Dario Amodei has called "fundamentally a crisis of trust" — with the decisive question, who decides what counts as public benefit, still unanswered.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/anthropic-says-its-committing-5-of-its-shares-to-a-public-benefit-fund/

**2. Disney will absorb most of Character.AI's staff through its bankruptcy sale**
Summary: A legal filing reported by Bloomberg shows Disney agreed to make employment offers to at least 60 Character.AI employees — the majority of the chatbot startup's workforce — at an estimated $60 million in annual compensation, on top of the $20 million it agreed to pay as stalking-horse bidder for some of the company's assets. Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 earlier this week, and its former CEO Karandeep Anand recently became Disney's first CTO. The structure is unusual: instead of buying the company, Disney is taking the IP and hiring the team, leaving the bankruptcy estate behind.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/disney-agrees-to-hire-the-majority-of-character-ais-workforce/

**3. DeepSeek prices its search and fetch tools at $0.75 per 1,000 requests**
Summary: DeepSeek published pricing for the Search and Fetch APIs that give its agents and RAG applications live web access — $0.75 per 1,000 successful requests, billed through the same bearer-auth DeepSeek API, days after it said the tools inside its Harness, Claude Code and Codex would stay free until billing starts October 31. The item hit 530 points on Hacker News, where developers benchmarked it against Exa (about $4), Brave ($5) and Kagi ($12) per thousand searches. Search is turning into a metered input for agentic coding, and DeepSeek is undercutting the incumbents by a wide margin.
Source: https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/search/ | https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50138531

**8. Together AI's HybridAgent reasons in latent space and acts in text**
Summary: Together AI researchers published Hybrid Reasoning Agents, which switch between continuous latent reasoning and text-based interaction inside a single generation process: a lightweight HybridLink moves between the two modes while a continuous working memory keeps context lossless across turns, and dedicated HybridSFT and HybridRL post-training teach the model when to think silently and when to act. Across 11 benchmarks at three model scales the authors report a 10.4% average gain over text-based and latent agent baselines, with 35.5–54.7% fewer tokens, 1.8–2.6x faster inference and 2.7–3.7x lower cost, with code released on GitHub. The economics matter more than the benchmark delta — token spend, not raw capability, is what limits long-running agents in production.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861
