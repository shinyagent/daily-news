**3. China's AI Mapped Its Entire Renewable Energy Grid — A World First**
Summary: Researchers from Peking University and Alibaba's DAMO Academy published a study in Nature that produced a complete, high-resolution, AI-generated inventory of China's entire wind and solar infrastructure — 319,972 solar facilities and 91,609 wind turbines. Using a deep-learning model trained on sub-meter satellite imagery (7.56TB), the team showed that solar-wind complementarity could substantially reduce generation variability if grid coordination moved from provincial to national scale. The dataset and code are publicly available.
Source: https://www.artificialintelligence-news.com/news/ai-energy-grid-mapping-china/

**7. AI-Driven Memory Shortage Is Repricing Consumer Electronics**
Summary: David Oks explains how the AI boom in high-bandwidth memory (HBM) is squeezing consumer electronics. Memory manufacturers have fixed wafer capacity split between DDR, LPDDR, and HBM — HBM's allocation jumped from 2% to an expected 20% by end of 2026, and HBM consumes 3x the wafer capacity per gigabyte. The result: sub-$100 smartphones are getting squeezed, and the memory companies' policy of under-provisioning means higher RAM prices for years. (Via Simon Willison's blog)
Source: https://davidoks.blog/p/ai-is-killing-the-cheap-smartphone

**10. Massive npm Supply Chain Attack Bypasses Sigstore's Provenance Verification**
