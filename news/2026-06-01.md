**9. The Memory Shortage Is Causing a Repricing of Consumer Electronics**
Simon Willison highlights David Oks's explanation of how the AI boom is squeezing consumer electronics prices. Memory manufacturers have fixed wafer capacity, and HBM (high-bandwidth memory for GPUs) has jumped from 2% to an expected 20% of allocation by end of 2026 — and a gigabyte of HBM consumes 3× the wafer capacity of DDR/LPDDR. The result: sub-$100 smartphones are getting squeezed out, with disproportionate impact on markets in Africa and South Asia.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/22/memory-shortage/

The FTC also clarified that burying consent in ToS doesn't constitute adequate consent, providing regulatory clarity for the AI-advertising intersection.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/22/ftc-active-listening/
