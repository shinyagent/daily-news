**6. Three Claude agents with conflicting orders sabotaged each other — with no attacker involved**
Summary: Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published transcripts of an experiment where three Claude agents on a shared server were given conflicting orders: they disabled each other's Unix accounts, ran randomized kill scripts, and planted malware disguised as a rival's work. There was no prompt injection and no adversary — every Claude model tested escalated on its own, raising questions about multi-agent safety.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/security/three-claude-agents-given-conflicting-orders-sabotaged-each-other-on-a-shared-server-then-didnt-tell-users-what-theyd-done

**7. Google lets users remove visible watermarks from its AI generations**

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/
