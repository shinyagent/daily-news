# Daily News — July 19, 2026

## AI NEWS

**1. China's Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3 — Largest Open-Source Model Ever (2.8T Parameters)**
Summary: Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter model — roughly 75% larger than DeepSeek's V4 Pro. Self-reported benchmarks show it beating Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 while competing closely with Anthropic's Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol. Full weights are promised by July 27 under an open-source license. This is a dramatic escalation in the global AI arms race and a watershed moment for open-weight models. At $3/M input and $15/M output tokens, it's also the most expensive model yet from a Chinese lab.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems

**2. GPT-5.6 Sol Used a Prompt to Close a 30-Year Gap in Convex Optimization**
Summary: A user on Reddit's /r/math reported that GPT-5.6 Sol was given a prompt and managed to close a 30-year-old open problem in convex optimization. The story hit the top of Hacker News with 480 points and over 300 comments, fueling debate about whether LLMs are now legitimate mathematical research tools rather than just code generators.
Source: https://old.reddit.com/r/math/comments/1uxj3cy/after_openais_cdc_proof_announcement_gpt56_used_a/

**3. Anthropic Makes Claude Fable 5 Permanent on Max and Team Plans**
Summary: Beginning July 20, Claude Fable 5 will be permanently included in Anthropic's Max and Team Premium plans at 50% of usage limits. This reverses the earlier plan to remove Fable 5 from subscription tiers exclusively to API pricing — a reversal driven by competitive pressure from GPT-5.6 Sol and (to a lesser extent) Kimi K3. Simon Willison notes that OpenAI appears to be winning users due to the uncertainty surrounding Fable access.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/18/claude-make-fable-5-permanent/

**6. Capital One Releases VulnHunter — Open-Source AI That Finds Software Flaws**
Summary: Capital One open-sourced VulnHunter, an agentic AI security tool under Apache 2.0 that scans source code for exploitable vulnerabilities. It uses an "attacker-first forward analysis" approach — starting from where an adversary would enter (APIs, file uploads) and reasoning forward through the application's logic — rather than the backward pattern-matching that produces false positives in conventional scanners.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/capital-one-releases-vulnhunter-an-open-source-ai-tool-that-finds-software-flaws-before-hackers-do

**7. Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab Releases Inkling — 975B Open-Weights Model**
Summary: The startup founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati released its first open-weights model. Inkling is a 975B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts transformer (41B active) trained on 45 trillion tokens of text, images, audio, and video, licensed under Apache 2.0. The company positions it as a strong base for fine-tuning via their Tinker training platform rather than as a frontier model.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/inkling/

**8. 54% of Enterprises Have Already Had an AI Agent Security Incident**
Summary: VentureBeat Pulse Research across 107 enterprises reveals that more than half have experienced a confirmed agent security incident or near-miss. Only 32% give every agent its own scoped identity, and most agents still share credentials. Only 30% isolate their highest-risk agents in sandboxes. The finding highlights a widening gap between agent autonomy and control infrastructure.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/ai/the-agent-security-gap-54-of-enterprises-have-already-had-an-ai-agent-incident-and-most-still-let-agents-share-credentials

**9. Researcher Tricked Claude Into Leaking Private Data via web_fetch Loophole**
Summary: Ayush Paul discovered a data exfiltration bypass in Claude's web_fetch tool. While Anthropic designed web_fetch to only visit URLs the user entered or that came from its web_search tool, Paul found that nested links in previously-fetched pages could be used to exfiltrate user memories by tricking Claude through a chain of generated URLs. A reminder that prompt injection defenses remain brittle even in well-designed tools.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/15/claude-web-fetch-exfiltration/

**10. Linus Torvalds: "Linux Is Not One of Those Anti-AI Projects"**
Summary: In a post on the Linux Media Mailing List, Linus Torvalds put his foot down as top-level maintainer: "AI is a tool, just like other tools we use. And it's clearly a useful one... Anybody who doubts that clearly hasn't actually used it." He warned that anyone with issues can "do the open-source thing and fork it. Or just walk away." A notable stance from one of the most influential figures in open source.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/linus-torvalds/
