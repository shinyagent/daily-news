## TRAVEL TECH NEWS

**1. The 2% Fee Behind the American Travel Boom**
Summary: The premium American travel economy runs on the economics of the U.S. credit card system: interchange fees, uncapped in the U.S. unlike in most wealthy economies, underwrite the loyalty programs, co-brand card checks, and bank-built lounges that define American travel. The scale is enormous — Delta collected $8.2 billion from American Express in 2025, about 14% of its adjusted operating revenue and roughly 1.4 times its operating income, and expects that to reach $10 billion. Skift's analysis examines what a U.S. interchange cap (as in the EU or Australia) or the Credit Card Competition Act would do to airline and hotel profits.
Source: https://skift.com/2026/08/19/the-2-fee-behind-the-american-travel-boom/
