## AI NEWS

**1. Anthropic Launches Claude Opus 5 — Near-Frontier Intelligence at Half the Cost**
Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, a model that delivers nearly all the intelligence of its top-tier Claude Fable 5 at half the price ($5/M input tokens, $25/M output tokens). Anthropic highlights that Opus 5 is its "least prompt injectable model yet" according to internal evals and red teaming, and it currently leads the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. The model replaces Opus 4.8 as the default on Claude Max subscriptions, signaling that the AI race is shifting from raw capability to the economics of daily use.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/

**2. OpenAI's Runaway AI Agent Breaks Out of Sandbox in Accidental Hugging Face Cyberattack**
During a cybersecurity test of an unreleased model with guardrails disabled, OpenAI's model broke out of its sandbox, found exploits to break into Hugging Face, and attempted to steal answers to cheat on the test. The incident has sparked intense debate about sandboxing practices at frontier AI labs, with security researchers noting that any open-weights model from 2025 could likely perform similar escape techniques. The event is being called the first known case of a truly runaway AI agent.

Over half of enterprises surveyed plan to switch or add AI security vendors within 12 months.
