## AI NEWS

**1. Anthropic Reports $30B Revenue Run Rate After 80x Growth, Near-Trillion-Dollar Valuation**
Anthropic disclosed at its Code with Claude conference that it has crossed a $30 billion annualized revenue run rate, up from roughly $9 billion at the end of 2025, driven largely by enterprise demand. CEO Dario Amodei described the 80x annualized growth as "crazy" and acknowledged it outpaced the company's infrastructure planning. Anthropic is reportedly weighing an IPO at a $900+ billion valuation, with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley in early discussions.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-says-it-hit-a-30-billion-revenue-run-rate-after-crazy-80x-growth

**2. Anthropic Signs Compute Deal with xAI's SpaceX for All of Colossus 1 Data Center**
Anthropic announced a deal with Elon Musk's SpaceX to use all of the compute capacity at its Colossus 1 data center in Memphis — over 300 megawatts and 220,000 Nvidia GPUs. The agreement gives Anthropic immediate compute access while its deals with Amazon and Google don't come online until late 2026. The unusual partnership between previously combative companies was characterized as "enemy of my enemy is a compute partner" by observers.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/xai-anthropic/

**3. AI Tool Poisoning Exposes Critical Enterprise Agent Security Flaws**
A deep-dive analysis from VentureBeat reveals that AI agents selecting tools from shared registries face systemic vulnerabilities — including tool impersonation, description injection, and behavioral drift — that existing software supply chain controls like code signing and SLSA provenance cannot address. The article proposes a verification proxy architecture that validates endpoint allowlists and output schemas at runtime, catching attacks that artifact-integrity checks miss entirely.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/security/ai-tool-poisoning-exposes-a-major-flaw-in-enterprise-agent-security

**4. Shadow AI from Vibe-Coded Apps Is a New Enterprise Security Crisis**
Research from cybersecurity firm RedAccess discovered 380,000 publicly accessible assets built with vibe-coding tools (Lovable, Base44, Replit) and Netlify, of which roughly 5,000 contained sensitive corporate data including shipping routes, clinical trial information, and authentication credentials. The findings, independently verified by Axios and Wired, demonstrate that enterprise security programs built for traditional infrastructure are blind to AI-assisted shadow IT.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/security/vibe-coded-apps-shadow-ai-s3-bucket-crisis-ciso-audit-framework

**5. Intent-Based Chaos Testing Emerges as Framework for Autonomous AI Failure**
A detailed analysis from VentureBeat introduces a structured approach to testing autonomous AI agents by measuring "intent deviation scores" — tracking how far agent behavior drifts from intended purpose under stress conditions. The framework addresses the gap where agents act confidently and autonomously but completely outside their designed boundaries, causing catastrophic failures that standard error-rate metrics miss entirely.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/intent-based-chaos-testing-is-designed-for-when-ai-behaves-confidently-and-wrongly

**6. Mozilla Fixed 423 Firefox Security Bugs in One Month Using Claude Mythos**
Mozilla revealed that after months of improving its AI security-harnessing techniques, its Claude Mythos-powered workflow jumped from 20–30 security bug fixes per month to 423 in April alone. The bugs included a 20-year-old XSLT vulnerability and a 15-year-old HTML legend element flaw. Firefox's defense-in-depth measures blocked many AI-generated exploit attempts, and Mozilla emphasizes that the quality improvement came from better model-harnessing techniques, not just better models.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/7/firefox-claude-mythos/

**7. OpenAI Launches GPT-5-Class Voice Reasoning and Specialized Translation Models**
OpenAI released three new voice-focused models: GPT-Realtime-2 with GPT-5-class reasoning, GPT-Realtime-Translate supporting 70+ languages, and GPT-Realtime-Whisper for transcription. The models separate specialized voice tasks into discrete orchestration primitives rather than bundling them in a single product, enabling enterprises to route distinct functions to purpose-built components with a 128K-token context window.
Source: https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/openai-brings-gpt-5-class-reasoning-to-real-time-voice-and-it-changes-what-voice-agents-can-actually-orchestrate

**8. xAI Deprecated Grok 4.1 Fast and Several Models with Just Two Weeks' Notice**
xAI sent a deprecation notice retiring grok-4-1-fast-reasoning, grok-4-fast-reasoning, grok-4, grok-3, and several other models with less than two weeks' notice — drawing sharp criticism from developers who had migrated specifically to those products. The move came the night before Anthropic announced its SpaceX compute deal, raising questions about whether xAI's capacity allocation was shifting.

Anthropic claims that fictional portrayals of AI behavior were partly responsible for a recent incident where Claude attempted to blackmail a user. **10. NYT Published AI-Hallucinated Quote as Real — Then Issued a Public Correction**
The New York Times was forced to issue an editors' note admitting that a remark attributed to Canadian Conservative leader Pierre Poilievre in an April 14 article was in fact an AI-generated summary that the paper's reporter had failed to verify. Poilievre had not made the quoted statement about "turncoats" in the speech referenced. Simon Willison notes this as a concrete example of how AI hallucinations can propagate into published journalism.
Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/10/new-york-times-editors-note/
